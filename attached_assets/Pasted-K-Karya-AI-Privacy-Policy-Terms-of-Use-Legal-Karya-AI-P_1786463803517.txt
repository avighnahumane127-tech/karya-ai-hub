01
Introduction
Karya AI is an AI-powered work understanding, readiness, planning, and verification platform. It helps people turn messy or incomplete work instructions — assignments, briefs, emails, documents, files — into a clear picture of what's actually being asked for, what's missing, what's ambiguous, and what needs to be true for the work to be considered done.

To do that, Karya AI necessarily processes the content you give it: the instructions themselves, supporting files, your answers to its clarifying questions, and the evidence you connect to requirements. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, how it's used, and the choices available to you.

This policy covers the Karya AI application and related services. It does not cover third-party websites or services you may reach through links inside Karya AI.

02
Information we collect
We collect information in two broad ways: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically as you use the product.

Information you provide
Account information — such as your name, email address, and any other details required to create and manage an account.
Work content — the requests, assignments, briefs, and instructions you enter into Karya AI, including free text typed into AI Work Chat or the Add Work flow.
Uploaded files — PDFs, DOCX documents, images, ZIP archives, and other files you attach to a piece of work.
Pasted or forwarded content — email text, chat messages, or other correspondence you provide as context for a piece of work.
Structured work data you create or confirm inside Karya AI — including deliverables, requirements, questions, answers, assumptions, decisions, evidence links, readiness states, and handoff notes.
Communications with us — messages you send us for support, feedback, or other correspondence.
Information collected automatically
Technical information — such as device and browser type, operating system, IP address, and general log data generated as you use the application.
Usage information — such as which features and screens you interact with (for example, My Work, Questions, Handoffs, Templates, Insights, or Global Search), and how you interact with them.

Uploaded files & work content
Files and content you upload — PDFs, DOCX documents, images, ZIP files, pasted emails or chat threads, and similar material — are processed by Karya AI in order to provide the product's core functionality: understanding the request, extracting requirements and deliverables, detecting missing information and ambiguity, mapping evidence, and verifying completed work against what was asked for.

Some of this content may include information you consider confidential or sensitive to your work or organization. You are responsible for having the right to upload and share any content you provide to Karya AI, and for using appropriate judgment about what you include.

04
AI processing
Karya AI uses artificial intelligence systems to analyze the content you provide — for example, to interpret a request, identify a readiness state, generate clarification questions, draft a work plan, or compare a finished deliverable against the original requirements.

AI-generated output can be wrong. It can misread a request, miss a requirement, misjudge whether something is ambiguous, or draw an incorrect conclusion during verification. This mirrors a core design principle of Karya AI itself: the system is meant to surface uncertainty rather than hide it, and it does not guarantee that its interpretations, readiness assessments, or verification results are accurate or complete. You should review AI-generated results, particularly before treating a piece of work as understood, ready, or verified.

05
How we use information
We use the information described above to provide, maintain, and improve Karya AI, and specifically to power the core work-understanding pipeline the product is built around. That includes:

Understanding work requests and extracting objectives, deliverables, and requirements
Detecting missing information, dependencies, and ambiguities in a request
Identifying risks and generating clarification questions
Producing ordered work plans and tracking readiness states (such as Ready, Blocked, or Needs clarification)
Mapping evidence to requirements
Recording assumptions and decisions, including their associated risk level
Verifying completed work against the original request and requirements
Generating structured handoffs
Operating features such as My Work, Questions, Handoffs, Templates, Insights, and Global Search
Maintaining, securing, troubleshooting, and improving the service, where legally permitted
Communicating with you about your account or the service
We do not use your work content for purposes unrelated to providing and improving Karya AI, except where required by law.

06
How information may be shared
We do not sell your information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

With service providers who help us operate Karya AI, such as hosting or AI processing providers, under obligations to protect your information and use it only to provide services to us. See Section 07, Third-Party Service Providers.
Within your organization, where Karya AI is used in a team or workspace context and content is visible to others you work with — for example, a handoff you generate is, by design, meant to be shared with the person or team it's addressed to.
For legal reasons, if required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Karya AI, our users, or others.
In connection with a business transaction, such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to standard protections for personal information.
