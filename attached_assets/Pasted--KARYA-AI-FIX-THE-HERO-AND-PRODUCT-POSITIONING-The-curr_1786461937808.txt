# KARYA AI — FIX THE HERO AND PRODUCT POSITIONING

The current hero is too narrow.

Do NOT position Karya AI primarily as:

> "Know what to do before you work."

That describes only one part of the product.

Karya AI is a much broader **AI work understanding, readiness, planning, verification, and handoff system**.

The hero must communicate that Karya AI understands work from beginning to end.

---

# CORE PRODUCT DEFINITION

Use this as the conceptual foundation of the entire landing page:

> **Karya AI understands the work, prepares it for execution, helps you navigate what needs to happen, and verifies whether the finished result actually satisfies the original request.**

The core lifecycle is:

**Understand → Prepare → Plan → Execute → Verify → Handoff**

The product is not simply:

**"AI that creates tasks."**

It is not simply:

**"AI that tells you what to do."**

It is not simply:

**"AI document analysis."**

It is an intelligent layer between **receiving work and completing work**.

---

# HERO HEADLINE

Replace the current headline with a more descriptive product-level headline.

Use:

## **AI that understands your work from request to completion.**

Supporting copy:

> **Give Karya AI an assignment, brief, email, document, image, folder, or other work instruction. It understands what is required, finds what's missing or unclear, builds the path forward, and verifies the finished result against the original request.**

This should be the primary hero messaging.
