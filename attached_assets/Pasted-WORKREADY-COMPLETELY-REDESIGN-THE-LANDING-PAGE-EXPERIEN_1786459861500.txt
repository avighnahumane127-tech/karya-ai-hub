27. MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT

The landing page must answer these questions without forcing the visitor to figure them out:

What is WorkReady?

An AI preflight and verification system for work.

What do I give it?

Assignments, briefs, PDFs, documents, files, emails, messages, and other work instructions.

What does it do?

It understands the request, finds missing information and ambiguity, creates an execution plan, and defines what completion requires.

What happens after I do the work?

Upload the result and WorkReady checks it against the original request.

Why is that useful?

Because people often start work without knowing exactly what is required — and finish work without knowing whether it actually satisfies the request.

What makes it different?

It maintains the chain:

Request → Deliverable → Requirement → Evidence → Verification

That chain is the core product concept.
